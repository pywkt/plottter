# Source Image Licenses

These reference images are bundled with Plottter for use as inputs to
image-based generators in the showcase script. All three are works whose
copyright has expired by age — they entered the public domain long ago and
have no licensing restrictions.

Per *Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel Corp.* (US, 1999) and the corresponding
Wikimedia Commons policy `PD-Art`, faithful 2D reproductions of public-domain
artworks do not generate a new copyright, so the digital images themselves
are also in the public domain.
